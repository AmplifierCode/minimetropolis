How to play 🏙️

Goal: grow a thriving city. Balance housing (R), shops (C) and industry (I), keep the lights on, and don't go broke.

  1. Build roads first. Zones only develop if they're within 3 tiles of a road.
  2. Add a power plant. Power flows through roads & buildings. Unpowered zones won't grow (look for the red ⚡).
  3. Zone land. Paint Residential, Commercial and Industrial areas. Buildings grow themselves over time when demand is positive.
  4. Watch the RCI meter (top right). Green bar up = that type is in demand, zone more of it. Bar down = oversupply.
  5. Money: taxes come in monthly from population & jobs; roads, power and services cost upkeep. Tune the tax slider — too high and approval drops. Broke? Raise the tax rate and bulldoze pricey Power/Police/Fire to cut upkeep until your monthly figure turns green — demolishing is free and refunds half the build cost.
  6. Services: Police & Fire stations and Parks raise land value, so nearby buildings grow taller.

Tip: click-and-drag to paint roads and zones quickly. Scroll to zoom in on the streets, right-drag (or arrow keys) to pan, 0 resets the view.
Keys: 1–9 pick tools, B bulldoze, ⌘/Ctrl+Z undo, Space pause.
